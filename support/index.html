Before you write

Most reports come down to a handful of known issues in the current version — alarms not firing reliably in the background, podcasts not being found, and battery drain. The FAQ covers what helps with each, and is honest about what can't be worked around.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: reportaproblem.apple.com. I can't issue them directly, but tell me if something went wrong — it's useful to know.
