Next Up

A podcast alarm clock for iPhone.

Wake up to the news, a meditation, a comedy show, or the voice of someone reading you a story — instead of a buzzer.

View on the App Store

What it does

Next Up was the first full-featured podcast alarm clock on the App Store. Set an alarm, pick a show, and wake up to it. Tailor every alarm: play newest episodes first for news, or start from the beginning for a serial you're catching up on. Set the volume, change the snooze length, skip past early ads and long intros. There's a built-in white noise generator for falling asleep, too.

Support

Getting help

Email support@nextupalarm.com and describe what happened — which alarm, what you expected, and what the app did instead. Screenshots help.

Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you can find them (Settings → General → About).

A note on the current version

Next Up hasn't been updated in some time, and on recent versions of iOS some things no longer work the way they did — most notably finding podcasts you've downloaded in Apple's Podcasts app, and alarms firing reliably in the background.

A full rebuild is underway on Apple's newer alarm APIs, which fix the underlying causes. If you've bought the app, your purchase carries over.
